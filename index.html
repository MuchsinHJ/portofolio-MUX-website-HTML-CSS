ABOUT ME

I am a Full Stack Developer with a passion for building modern and scalable web applications. With over 2 years of experience, I have worked on various projects ranging from startups to enterprise companies.

I work in JavaScript and TypeScript, with C++ when something needs to run close to the metal. On the frontend I build with React.js, styled in Tailwind CSS or Bootstrap over semantic HTML5 and CSS3. On the backend I write Node.js and Express.js services behind a RESTful API, with PostgreSQL and Prisma ORM holding the data.

I ship to a VPS, Vercel, or Railway depending on what the app needs, keep everything in Git and GitHub, verify endpoints in Postman, and design interfaces in Figma before writing a line of markup.
